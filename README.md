# NullSpectre - Your space. Your signal. Protected.

**NullSpectre** describes itself as defensive sensing technology: WiFi-based detection of unauthorized devices, rogue access points and transmitting cameras, on a network you control. In its own words: no camera, no microphone, and it detects devices, not people.

<p align="center"><a href="https://nullspectre.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme"><b>See how it works &rarr;</b></a></p>

## How it works (as NullSpectre describes it)
1. **Place** the sensor anywhere in the space.
2. **Connect** - the sensor joins your WiFi and maps the network: every device and access point transmitting in range.
3. **Protect** - NullSpectre learns which devices belong to your network and alerts when a device that does not belong appears.

## What it does not detect
In NullSpectre's own words, it does not detect analog RF transmitters, cellular-only (GSM/LTE) devices, Bluetooth-only devices, wired cameras, or recorders that write to a memory card without transmitting. It is not a replacement for a full professional TSCM sweep.

## Pricing (as of 2026-11-23; current prices: https://nullspectre.com/)
Hardware kits with a yearly subscription, for example Home Starter: $79 kit, then $59 a year (one sensor). Larger kits: Home, Pro and Enterprise. When the subscription ends, the hardware stays yours; monitoring and alerts pause.
